How a run works

One run is one fiscal year.

16 workdays in 4 quarters. The quota grows every day, and three times a year the Board shows up in person.

  1. 9:00 AM

    The workday

    Tap workers to motivate them. Wake the sleepers, break up the gossip, reboot the jammed printer. Overclock a machine when you're behind.

  2. 5:00 PM

    The Daily Report

    Revenue rolls in desk by desk. Hit the quota and the Board keeps the money and pays you an allowance. Miss it and HR prints your letter.

  3. After hours

    The shop

    Buy robots, hire people, sign policies. Drop a robot on someone's desk and it replaces them.

  4. Days 4 · 8 · 12

    Board Meeting

    The quota jumps ×1.25 and one Board member's rule runs the day. The Consultant takes your weakest desk and bills you $50. Survive it and pick a perk.

  5. Day 16

    Final Performance Review

    CORA, the company AI, runs your last review. She has her own ideas about who is essential.

R&D Catalog

Secret recipes

Drop a robot on the right worker and they fuse. Some fusions are pure machine, others are a person with upgrades. There are ten recipes, and one you haven't found looks like a plain replacement until you try it.

Form HR-404

Fired by $3

Miss one quota and the run ends with your Termination Letter. You keep the Shareholder Value you earned for permanent upgrades, copy the seed to replay the same office, and reapply.

The company AI

CORA is watching.

She's on from day one. Every machine you add opens her eye a little wider.

From the second quarter she sends Proposals: a free robot, printers that copy themselves. Each one is a great deal, and each one adds Control. On day 16, that Control decides how hard her Final Review gets.

Somebody always gets fired in the end.
